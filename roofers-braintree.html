Spray foam removal Braintree

Spray Foam Removal in Braintree

Spray foam removal is one of the most important services for homeowners who are planning to sell, remortgage or prepare their roof for a property survey. Spray foam can make it harder for surveyors and lenders to inspect roof timbers, rafters and ventilation, which is why many homeowners choose to have it carefully removed.

Our wording is intentionally careful: removal cannot guarantee mortgage approval or a sale, but it can help prepare your roof for inspection, support the sale or remortgage process and allow accessible roof timbers to be more clearly assessed.
