When to replace your roof

When Should You Replace Your Roof?

Sometimes a roof repair is the right option. In other cases, a full replacement may offer better value and longer-term peace of mind. A survey helps you understand whether the roof can be repaired or whether replacement would be the better route.

  • Repeated leaks after previous repairs
  • Large areas of cracked, missing or slipped tiles
  • Ageing roof coverings nearing the end of their life
  • Sagging, uneven or visibly tired roof areas
  • Worn felt, battens or roofline details
  • Planning property upgrades, sale or renovation work

Repair or Replace?

We do not push a new roof where a practical repair is enough. Our survey is designed to help you understand the condition of your roof and choose a sensible option based on the issue, age of the roof and your plans for the property.

If a repair is more suitable, we can point you towards our roof repair service. If replacement is the better long-term option, we can provide a clear quote for the work required.
