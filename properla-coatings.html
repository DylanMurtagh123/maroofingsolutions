Frequently Asked Questions

What is ProPERLA coating?

ProPERLA coating is a specialist roof or exterior wall coating designed to help protect suitable surfaces from weather, moisture absorption and staining while remaining breathable.

Do you install ProPERLA coatings in Braintree?

Yes. MA Roofing Solutions Ltd provides ProPERLA roof and wall coatings in Braintree and across nearby Essex areas.

Can ProPERLA be used on roofs and walls?

Yes, depending on the surface and condition. We can advise whether roof coating, masonry cream or another ProPERLA product is suitable after a survey.

Is ProPERLA coating breathable?

Yes. ProPERLA coatings are designed to repel water while allowing suitable surfaces to breathe, helping avoid trapping vapour within the structure.

Can exterior wall coating help protect against damp?

Exterior wall coating can help reduce water absorption through suitable masonry, but any existing damp should be assessed properly so the cause is understood before coating.
