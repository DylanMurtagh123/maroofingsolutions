Roof cleaning

Roof Cleaning for Moss, Algae and Built-Up Dirt

Roofs take the full force of the weather. Over the years, moss, algae, lichen and dirt can build up across tiles and roof surfaces, leaving the property looking tired. A professional roof clean can make a noticeable difference to the overall look of the home.

Because every roof is different, we always assess the roof condition first. We look at access, tile condition, pitch, moss build-up and any visible concerns before recommending a suitable cleaning method.
