Leaking roof repairs

Leaking Roof Repairs After Heavy Rain

A leaking roof can quickly cause stress for homeowners, especially after heavy rain or strong winds. Common causes include slipped tiles, cracked tiles, damaged ridge tiles, worn lead flashing, blocked valleys, flat roof defects or storm damage.

Our emergency roof repair service is designed to identify the source of the issue and help protect your property. Where a full repair cannot be completed immediately, we can advise on the safest temporary options before completing a longer-term fix.

Urgent Roof Safety Concerns

If part of your roof looks loose, unstable or damaged, avoid climbing onto the roof and contact a professional roofer for advice.

What to do first

What Should You Do If Your Roof Is Leaking?

If you notice a leak, the first priority is safety. Avoid climbing onto the roof, entering unsafe loft spaces or touching electrics near water. If possible, move furniture and belongings away from the leak and use a bucket or container to catch dripping water.

Take photos of the visible damage where it is safe to do so, then contact a local roofer for advice. The sooner the roof is inspected, the easier it may be to limit further water damage and plan the right repair.
