Spray foam removal Great Dunmow

Spray Foam Removal for Surveys, Sales and Remortgage Concerns

Spray foam insulation can create concerns when a home is being sold, remortgaged or surveyed because it may make roof timbers, rafters and ventilation details harder to inspect. We remove spray foam from loft spaces and roof timbers where removal is the right route for the property.

We avoid overclaiming: spray foam removal cannot guarantee a sale, mortgage approval or remortgage approval. It can help prepare your roof for inspection, support survey conversations and allow accessible roof timbers to be more clearly assessed.
