Spray foam removal Finchingfield

Spray Foam Removal for Surveys, Sales and Remortgage Concerns

Some Finchingfield homeowners contact us because spray foam insulation has created questions during a sale, remortgage or property survey. In older cottages and rural homes, spray foam can make rafters, roof timbers and ventilation details harder for surveyors to assess, especially where visibility inside the roof space is already limited.

We remove spray foam carefully to help prepare the roof structure for inspection and allow accessible timbers to be more clearly assessed. We do not promise mortgage approval or a guaranteed sale, but removal can support the inspection and survey process.

  • Loft spray foam removal in Finchingfield
  • Removal from rafters and roof timbers
  • Help preparing the roof structure for inspection
